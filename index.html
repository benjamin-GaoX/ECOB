After NPS selection, wall thickness is recalculated using the commercial OD. Cost, GHG and pumping are then evaluated once; they are not used to search for another NPS.
View method workflow

1 · ECOB endpoint

Predict the lowest-transport-cost diameter and map it once to the economic baseline commercial pipe.

2 · LCOB endpoint

Predict the lowest-lifecycle-GHG diameter and map it once to the climate endpoint commercial pipe.

3 · Practical NPS options

Evaluate each standard NPS lying strictly between the two endpoint pipes once; no additional diameter optimization is performed.

4 · Carbon-value ranking

Compare transport cost, lifecycle GHG, break-even carbon value, and carbon-adjusted cost across the displayed commercial options.

View key assumptions
Commercialization: one-shot mapping to the nearest standard OD, then selection of the minimum compliant wall schedule.
Hydraulics: common EVR framework with simplified rapid-screening hydraulics and a fixed friction factor.
LCA scope: streamlined split dominated by welded steel pipe and pumping electricity.
Carbon-adjusted cost: transport cost plus monetized lifecycle GHG at the user-selected carbon value.
Use case: rapid screening only; final projects still require case-specific hydraulic, mechanical, cost, and LCA verification.
