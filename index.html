Diameter and cost parity plots use tabulated results from Supplementary Table S5 (rounded for reporting). Accuracy statistics are from the published analysis; rare stress cases can have higher deviations (up to 22.6% in cost). Shaded band: ±10% around the reference.

Scope & limitations

ECOB is intended for preliminary screening of steady-state, onshore, dense-phase CO₂ pipelines within the studied parameter ranges. Project-specific hydraulic, mechanical, cost and lifecycle verification remains necessary. Read method & limitations.
