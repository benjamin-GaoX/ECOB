Full-process validation

166 internal out-of-sample cases, compared with detailed Aspen Plus V14 hydraulic–economic optimisation under a common modelling framework. This is not independent field validation.

Diameter and cost parity plots use tabulated results from Supplementary Table S5 (rounded for reporting). Median and P90 absolute percentage errors are calculated from the reported error columns in Supplementary Table S5 (rounded values; linear percentile interpolation). A zero median cost error reflects the precision of the tabulated errors, not exact equality. Rare stress cases reach 22.6% in cost. Shaded band: ±10% around the reference.

Scope & limitations

ECOB is intended for preliminary screening of steady-state, onshore, dense-phase CO₂ pipelines within the studied parameter ranges. Project-specific hydraulic, mechanical, cost and lifecycle verification remains necessary. Read method & limitations.
